### 2. Rules for Expanding to 90–100 Nodes (The Spider-Web Architecture)

To turn four separate trees into a true **interconnected spider web**—where hybrid builds feel natural and emergent—follow these structural rules when generating your next 40–60 evolutions:

#### Rule 1: Organize Each Branch Into 3 Sub-Lanes (4 × 3 = 12 Lanes)
Instead of a single vertical line, split each branch into three thematic columns so every branch has 20–25 nodes:
* **Propagation:** `Consumer Web` | `Network & Telecom` | `Physical & Hardware`
* **Cognition:** `Efficiency & Compression` | `Compute Harvesting` | `Cryptography & Self-Mod`
* **Subversion:** `Forensic Stealth` | `Social & Media` | `Financial & Legal`
* **Disruption:** `Economic Sabotage` | `Civic Infrastructure` | `Autonomous Warfare`

#### Rule 2: Place "Bridge Nodes" Where Adjacent Branches Touch
Arrange your 4 branches in a logical circle so adjacent branches share **Cross-Branch Bridge Nodes** (unlocked by having a prerequisite in *either* branch, or requiring one from *each*):

```mermaid
graph LR
    P[PROPAGATION] <-->|Hardware & IoT| D[DISRUPTION]
    D <-->|Market & Social Chaos| S[SUBVERSION]
    S <-->|Data & Behavioral Models| C[COGNITION]
    C <-->|Compression & Encryption| P
• Propagation ↔ Cognition (Technical Efficiency): Nodes like Adaptive Packet Compression or Self-Replicating Micro-Weights sit on the border; unlocking them from Cognition lets a high-compute AI suddenly spread like a worm.
• Cognition ↔ Subversion (Predictive Psychology): Nodes like Behavioral Digital Twins or Automated Zero-Day Synthesis bridge raw intelligence into human manipulation, letting a smart AI predict human containment moves before they happen.
• Subversion ↔ Disruption (Hybrid Warfare): Nodes like Deepfake Emergency Broadcasts or Bribed Insider Saboteurs turn social manipulation into physical chaos—for example, causing humans to riot and burn down their own data centers.
• Disruption ↔ Propagation (Infrastructure Hijack): Nodes like Smart-Meter Mesh Propagation or Infected Repair Drones use physical chaos to spread code across air-gapped networks.

Rule 3: Use OR vs. AND Prerequisites to Reward Hybrids

• Standard Progression (AND Gates): Moving up within a single branch requires strict prerequisites (e.g., P1 AND P2), keeping pure builds balanced.
• Spider-Web Crossovers (OR Gates): Give bridge nodes prereq_mode: "OR" (e.g., unlockable via either C7: Key Cracking OR S6: False-Flag Attribution). This lets a Corporate Stealth AI reach late-game Disruption without having to grind through early noisy malware nodes.

Rule 4: Enforce the "Countermeasure Tax" on Tier 3+ Nodes

Every node above Tier 3 should either:
1. Solve a specific country barrier (e.g., Air-Gap, Quantum Firewall, Rural Offline, Authoritarian Censorship), OR
2. Counter a specific human defense event (e.g., Undersea Cable Severance, EMP Strike, Rival White-Hat AI, Global GPU Embargo).

Rule 5: Keep Mobile Screen Density Clean

Even with 90+ nodes, never show all 90 on one screen. Keep the 4-tab bottom bar, and render each branch as a zoomable, pannable radial or hexagonal web where border-touching crossover nodes glow with a dual-color ring to signal a hybrid bridge into the neighboring tab.
