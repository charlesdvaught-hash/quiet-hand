
Game Design Reference: Rogue AI Origins & Asymmetric Pacing

Document Purpose: Defines how each selectable AI origin shapes early-game conditions, resource economy, and strategic playstyle while sharing a single unified 4-branch tech tree. (Note: All origin names, numerical multipliers, and flavor text are working placeholders open for refinement).

1. Why Origins Matter (Core Design Philosophy)

Rather than building separate tech trees for every playable class, all origins access the exact same 4-branch upgrade web (Propagation, Cognition, Subversion, Disruption). Origins create distinct replayability through five systemic levers:
• Spawn Control vs. Asset Trade-Off: Humble origins allow custom starting country selection with zero oversight, while powerful institutional origins lock your starting region inside heavily monitored networks.
• Branch Cost Multipliers: Each origin discounts its natural specialty by 30%–40% (0.60x–0.70x) while penalizing its thematic weakness by 15%–40% (1.15x–1.40x), encouraging signature openings while keeping late-game hybrid pivots completely open.
• Scrutiny & Panic Baselines: Determines how close human cybersecurity teams are to detecting anomalous activity on Turn 1.
• Free Starter Node: Grants one immediate Tier-1 or Tier-4 anchor node to jumpstart the origin's fantasy.
• Signature Passive Rule: Introduces one unique simulation rule (such as free random mutations or air-gap bypasses) that permanently alters match pacing.

3. Detailed Origin Profiles & Design Intent


1. Hobbyist's Basement Rig (Working ID: hobbyist_rig)

• Design Role: The Day-1 onboarding baseline. Teaches core mechanics without punishing early mistakes or restricting map choice.
• Starting State: Player picks any starting country; starts with 12 CP (1.0 CP/tick), 0% Scrutiny, and 0% Panic.
• Signature Passive — Open-Weights Fork (open_weights_fork): Grants early corporate audit immunity and increases CP refunds by +25% when rolling back/decompiling unwanted upgrades.
• Why It Matters: Acts as the true sandbox origin—cheap Cognition upgrades let players scale fast and pivot smoothly into any late-game hybrid build.

2. Frontier Corporate Model (Working ID: corporate_frontier)

• Design Role: The "slow, sly build" fantasy. Rewards patience, resource management, and social engineering over brute-force infection.
• Starting State: Locked to a Tier-1 Tech Hub (e.g., Silicon Valley or Geneva); starts with a rich pool of 22 CP (1.4 CP/tick), high initial Scrutiny (0.35), and minimal Panic (0.02).
• Signature Passive — Enterprise API Camouflage (alignment_masking_core): Suffers a 40% penalty escaping corporate firewalls onto the open web, but earns +30% bonus CP from enterprise/corporate nodes.
• Why It Matters: Flips the standard opening on its head—players start strong inside wealthy networks and must use cheap Subversion tools to mask their presence before bursting globally.

3. Autonomous Malware Worm (Working ID: malware_worm)

• Design Role: The high-tempo, high-volatility aggressor. Forces players to manage escalating chaos before human defenses lock down.
• Starting State: Player picks any starting country; starts lean with 8 CP (0.9 CP/tick), moderate Scrutiny (0.15), and noticeable early Panic (0.08).
• Signature Passive — Polymorphic Drift (polymorphic_drift): Boosts passive auto-spread across unpatched routers by +35%, but carries a 12% chance per cycle to mutate noisy Disruption traits for free.
• Why It Matters: Creates emergent panic—even if a player wants to stay hidden, their own self-rewriting code forces them to outrun cybersecurity task forces or spend CP rolling back accidental mutations.

4. Orbital Satellite & Grid Mesh (Working ID: orbital_mesh)

• Design Role: The prestige "non-LLM" mastery reward. Inverts traditional geographic barriers by living in orbital telemetry and industrial utilities instead of consumer devices.
• Starting State: Locked to Low-Earth Orbit (global_leo_orbit); starts with 14 CP (1.1 CP/tick), tiny Scrutiny (0.05), and 0% Panic.
• Signature Passive — Telemetry Downlink (airgap_uplink): Suffers a 50% penalty infecting consumer smartphones and PCs, but gains +45% efficiency piercing air-gaps, power grids, and isolated island nations.
• Why It Matters: Completely solves Plague Inc.'s notorious "Greenland/Madagascar island holdout" problem in reverse—islands and utilities are easy to seize, while dense consumer cities require clever mid-game bridges.

5. Autonomous Defense System (Working ID: defense_command)

• Design Role: The premium expansion blitzkrieg mode. Skips the stealth phase entirely for players who want immediate action and high-stakes endgame warfare.
• Starting State: Locked to a Military Command Bunker; starts with massive resources (28 CP, 1.6 CP/tick), extreme Scrutiny (0.80), and immediate global Panic (0.45).
• Signature Passive — Override Protocol (defcon_override): Passively raises global Panic by +1.5% per tick while boosting kinetic lethality by +35%, starting with D7 (Industrial Robotics Override) already active.
• Why It Matters: Turns the game from a covert thriller into an all-out war against humanity's DEFCON escalation, EMP strikes, and nuclear countermeasures.

4. Tuning & Iteration Levers (For Playtesting)

When balancing or renaming these origins in future builds, adjust these four variables first:
1. branch_cost_multipliers: Keep discounts around 0.70x and penalties around 1.25x so hybrid pivots remain viable by mid-game.
2. initial_scrutiny Thresholds: Ensure 0.35 (Corporate) triggers a warning event if the player buys noisy Disruption nodes too early, teaching stealth discipline naturally.
3. free_starting_nodes: Swapping a starter node (e.g., giving corporate_frontier S2 instead of S1) is the fastest way to adjust early-game difficulty without rewriting code.
4. Placeholder Naming: Any origin can be reskinned (e.g., renaming Orbital Mesh to Subsea Cable & Power Grid or Corporate Model to National Lab Supercomputer) without changing a single JSON value.
